The gang's food choices are questionable, so I tried making some of them at home. Here are the recipes and my honest reviews. Got your own take? Leave a review at the bottom of the page.

Rum Ham

As seen in "The Gang Goes to the Jersey Shore" (Season 7). Serves 8 to 10.

Ingredients

  • 1 fully cooked bone-in ham (8 to 10 lb)
  • 2 cups dark rum
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup pineapple juice
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • Whole cloves (optional)

Steps

  1. Score the ham in a diamond pattern and press a clove into each diamond.
  2. Place the ham in a large bag or pot, pour in 1 1/2 cups of rum, and refrigerate overnight, turning it once.
  3. Heat the oven to 325°F (165°C). Move the ham to a roasting pan with the rum.
  4. Bake about 15 minutes per pound, basting every 30 minutes.
  5. Simmer the brown sugar, pineapple juice, mustard and remaining rum into a glaze. Brush it on for the last 20 minutes.

My Review

★★★★★Rated 5 out of 5

Honestly delicious. The rum cooks down into a sweet, caramel-like glaze. Unlike Frank, I recommend not taking it out into the ocean.

Milk Steak

Charlie's favorite food, ordered "boiled over hard." Serves 2.

Ingredients

  • 2 sirloin steaks (about 8 oz each)
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, smashed
  • Salt and pepper
  • A handful of jelly beans (raw, of course)

Steps

  1. Season the steaks with salt and pepper.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the steaks for 2 minutes per side.
  3. Lower the heat, add the garlic and pour in the milk.
  4. Simmer gently for about 10 minutes, until the steak is cooked through.
  5. Serve with a side of your finest jelly beans.

My Review

★★☆☆☆Rated 2 out of 5

The steak itself came out surprisingly tender, but the jelly beans did not help. Fun to make once for a watch party, but I won't be ordering it again.

Fight Milk (Safe Version)

Mac and Charlie's boozy, dairy-based protein drink for bodyguards. My version skips the vodka and crow eggs. Serves 1.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1 banana
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup ice

Steps

  1. Add everything to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth, about 30 seconds.
  3. Pour into a tall glass and yell "Caw! Caw!" (optional but encouraged).

My Review

★★★★☆Rated 4 out of 5

A genuinely good post-workout shake. It won't turn you into a bodyguard, but it tastes way better than the original sounds.
