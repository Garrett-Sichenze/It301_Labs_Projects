Meet the Gang

Five friends (and one dad) who own and "run" Paddy's Pub. Each one is awful in their own special way. Here's who they are and the actors behind them.

The Main Five

Charlie Kelly

The wild card

Played by
Charlie Day
Job at Paddy's
Janitor (doing "Charlie work")

Charlie can barely read, sleeps in a room with Frank, and is obsessed with the Waitress, who wants nothing to do with him. He's the heart of the gang and is often the most creative, writing a whole musical and claiming to be an expert in "bird law."

Charlie Day is also known for Horrible Bosses, Pacific Rim and voicing Luigi in The Super Mario Bros. Movie.

Dennis Reynolds

The Golden God

Played by
Glenn Howerton
Job at Paddy's
Bartender and self-appointed leader

Dennis is Dee's twin brother and the most vain member of the gang. He believes he is a perfect specimen and loses his temper the moment anyone disagrees with him. His calm voice turning into screaming is one of the show's best running jokes.

Glenn Howerton also starred in A.P. Bio and the movie BlackBerry.

Mac

The "bouncer"

Played by
Rob Mac
Job at Paddy's
Head of security (he says)

Mac's real name is Ronald McDonald. He thinks he is a karate master and a tough guy, but he's mostly bluffing. Over the years he has gone through big changes, including coming out as gay in later seasons.

Rob Mac (formerly Rob McElhenney) created the show. He also co-created Mythic Quest and co-owns the Welsh soccer club Wrexham AFC.

Dee Reynolds

Sweet Dee (the "bird")

Played by
Kaitlin Olson
Job at Paddy's
Bartender and waitress

Dee dreams of being a famous actress or comedian, but the rest of the gang constantly insults her. She is just as scheming as the guys and usually ends up on the losing end of the gang's plans.

Kaitlin Olson is known for her physical comedy and now also stars in the crime show High Potential.

Frank Reynolds

The Warthog

Played by
Danny DeVito
Joined in
Season 2

Frank is Dennis and Dee's legal father, a rich businessman who gave up his normal life to live like an animal with Charlie. He bankrolls most of the gang's worst ideas.

Danny DeVito is a Hollywood legend known for Taxi, Matilda and playing the Penguin in Batman Returns.

Familiar Faces

  • The Waitress

    Charlie's longtime crush. Played by Mary Elizabeth Ellis, Charlie Day's real-life wife.

  • Rickety Cricket

    A former priest whose life gets ruined by the gang again and again. Played by David Hornsby.

  • Artemis

    Dee's wild actress friend who is up for anything. Played by Artemis Pebdani.
