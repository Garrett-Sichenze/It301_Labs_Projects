Behind the Scenes

How three struggling actors turned a homemade pilot into the longest-running live-action sitcom in American TV history.

From Camcorder to Record Breaker

  1. 2004

    Rob Mac, Glenn Howerton and Charlie Day shoot a pilot on a home video camera with a tiny budget. It was originally about struggling actors in Los Angeles.

  2. 2005

    FX picks up the show, moves the story to Philadelphia, and premieres it on August 4.

  3. 2006

    Danny DeVito joins the cast as Frank in Season 2.

  4. 2009

    "The Nightman Cometh" musical goes on a live stage tour with the original cast.

  5. 2013

    The show moves from FX to its new sister channel, FXX.

  6. 2021

    Season 15 makes it the longest-running live-action sitcom in U.S. history.

  7. 2026

    Season 18 premieres on August 17 with 10 episodes, up from 8 in recent seasons.

On Location

Even though the show is set in South Philadelphia, most of it is filmed in Los Angeles on sound stages and city streets made to look like Philly. The crew uses establishing shots of real Philadelphia streets and landmarks to set each scene.

The cast has filmed in Philadelphia for some episodes, and fans visiting the city can explore the real neighborhoods the gang "lives" in. Plan a trip with Visit Philadelphia.

Fun Facts

Real-life couples

Rob Mac is married to Kaitlin Olson (Dee), and Charlie Day is married to Mary Elizabeth Ellis, who plays the Waitress.

"Fat Mac"

Rob Mac gained about 50 pounds for Season 7 as a joke about sitcom actors getting in shape over time. Years later he got extremely muscular for Season 13.

That cheerful theme song

The upbeat opening music is "Temptation Sensation," a piece of stock library music. It's funny because it's the total opposite of how the gang behaves.

A new name

In 2025, Rob McElhenney started going by Rob Mac, a shorter version of his last name.

The podcast

Since 2021, Charlie, Glenn and Rob have hosted The Always Sunny Podcast, where they rewatch old episodes and share stories from filming.
