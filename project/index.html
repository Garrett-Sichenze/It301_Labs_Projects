Meet the Gang

Today's Special: The Premise

Charlie, Mac, Dennis, Dee and Frank (known as "the gang") own Paddy's Pub, a dive bar that almost never has customers. Instead of running the bar, they spend every episode chasing get-rich-quick schemes, picking fights with each other and making every situation worse. The joke is that nobody ever learns a lesson.

  • Many episode titles start with "The Gang..." (like "The Gang Buys a Boat").
  • The characters are selfish on purpose; the show makes fun of their bad behavior.
  • It mixes dark humor, physical comedy and surprisingly good musical episodes.
