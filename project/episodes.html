Why They're Great

Season S4

The Gang Solves the Gas Crisis

The gang buys a truck full of gasoline to sell door-to-door, and every member has a completely different (and terrible) plan for how it should go.

Why I love it

It's the perfect example of the gang's "plans" falling apart in the funniest way possible.

Season S4

The Nightman Cometh

Charlie writes and directs a full musical that is secretly a giant marriage proposal to the Waitress. Nobody understands what it's about, least of all the cast.

Why I love it

The songs are actually catchy, and it was popular enough to become a real live stage show.

Season S5

The D.E.N.N.I.S. System

Dennis explains his step-by-step "system" for getting women to fall for him, and the rest of the gang tries to copy it with disastrous results.

Why I love it

It shows exactly how unhinged Dennis is, and Mac's and Charlie's versions are hilarious.

Season S7

Chardee MacDennis: The Game of Games

The gang plays their own homemade board game that mixes trivia, physical challenges and emotional torture, and Frank has to learn the rules the hard way.

Why I love it

It's a whole episode of the characters being petty and competitive in one room.

Season S10

Charlie Work

A health inspector visits Paddy's on the same day the gang is running a scheme, so Charlie has to keep the whole bar from failing inspection.

Why I love it

Much of it is filmed to look like one long continuous shot, which is really impressive.

Season S13

Mac Finds His Pride

Mac struggles to explain who he really is to his father, so he expresses it through an interpretive dance in the season's final episode.

Why I love it

It's the show at its most surprising and sincere, and the dance is beautiful.
